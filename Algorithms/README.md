# Design & Analysis of Algorithms

Study and analysis of algorithmic efficiency, computational complexity, and fundamental design paradigms developed as part of coursework at NYU Tandon.

## Covered Topics

- **Asymptotic Analysis**: Big-O, Big-$\Omega$, and Big-$\Theta$ notations, recurrence relations, and the Master Theorem.
- **Divide and Conquer**: Merge Sort, Quick Sort, binary search variations, and median finding algorithms.
- **Dynamic Programming**: Optimal substructure, memoization vs. tabulation, knapsack problems, longest common subsequences (LCS), and graph path optimization.
- **Greedy Algorithms**: Interval scheduling, Huffman coding, minimum spanning trees (Kruskal's and Prim's algorithms).
- **Graph Algorithms**: Breadth-First Search (BFS), Depth-First Search (DFS), Dijkstra's shortest path, Bellman-Ford, and topological sorting.
- **Intractability & Complexity**: P vs. NP, NP-Completeness, and polynomial-time reductions.
